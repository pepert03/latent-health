How it works

The loop

Not a kit, not an app: it's what connects the medical record, the patient at home and the doctor.

  1. 01 · the practice list

    The medical record

    Every night it scans the doctor's 1,500 patients: age, diagnoses, treatments and any lab results on file.

  2. 02 · the engine

    How far to trust each measurement

    • Albuminuria: Bayesian filter
    • Blood: virtual panel
    • CV risk: real outcomes
    • Rhythm, EF, diabetes

  3. 03 · at home

    Only what's missing

    • Urine strip and app
    • Watch: pulse and ECG
    • BP monitor, for a week
    • Glucose at the pharmacy
    • Lab tube by mail

  4. 04 · the clinic

    One list, not a hundred alerts

    • Act: what's clear but not on record
    • Measure: the most informative test
    • Order, note and code, pre-written

The kit

  1. It shows up without asking.

    A small box, like a medicine pack. Your health center sends it when your record shows you're missing the test, and it fits in the mailbox.

  2. Three strips, a cup and a color card.

    Each strip comes in its own pouch. The cup unfolds and goes in the trash after. The color card is where the camera reads the strip.

  3. The app reads the strip on the card.

    The corner marks and color patches correct for your home's lighting. If there's still doubt, it asks for a lab tube: it goes back to the lab in the same box, postage prepaid.

  4. Add-ons, only if you need them.

    Your watch, if you already have one, for pulse and a 30-second ECG. A loaner BP monitor for a week if your blood pressure came out high. Glucose: one reading at the pharmacy.

The app

It tells you what to do, not a number.

  1. A nudge on the right dayThe model sets the date, based on your previous tests.
  2. It checks first, in the chatHard exercise, fever, your period or burning: if anything applies, the test waits.
  3. It reads the strip on the cardThe marks and color patches correct for your home's lighting before reading the two pads.
  4. A clear answerAll done, repeat tomorrow or send the tube. Only borderline results repeat.
  5. Your logEvery test narrows the doubt. You see your estimated value and how it changes.
  6. Your watch, connectedApple Watch, Galaxy Watch, Pixel, Fitbit or Garmin: daily pulse and a 30 s ECG. No watch? The camera works.
  7. An assistant that explainsAsk it about your tests. It guides; it doesn't diagnose.
  8. Your doctor, one message awayWith your tests attached, whenever you want to talk to a person.
