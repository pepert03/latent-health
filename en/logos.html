Seven ideas for the logo

Each proposal is a different concept. For each one: the app icon, the same icon at 32 px as on a phone's home screen, the logotype on white and on ink, and the one-color version. The strip palette and wide Archivo, with the word converted to outlines. Click any image to open its SVG.

01

Chart

Builds on the current logo: the four squares of the albumin chart, from yellow to teal, because reading the strip at home means matching its color against this scale.

02

Iceberg

What causes symptoms is only the tip; the strip measures what lies below the waterline, and the deeper it goes, the further along the color scale.

03

Drop

Where the drop touches the pad, color appears: one drop of urine at home reveals what was latent.

04

Home

The pad under a roof, because the test is done at home with no appointment; in negative it is the brand button, ink with its lime square.

05

Rhythm

Measuring several times: each measurement sits inside the previous one and the uncertainty narrows to the result, in Albers-style nested squares using the strip scale.

06

Strips

The L and the H are made of strips and carry the test's two pads: albumin at the foot of the L and creatinine on the crossbar of the H.

07

Weight

The word goes from a thin stroke to a black one without changing typeface (Archivo, weight 300 to 900): the latent becomes visible.
