# Virtual panel: what can be estimated without a blood test

Generated by `scripts/fit_panel.py`. Data: NHANES 2009-2018 (lab tests, urine and clinical history, 26,762 adults) and NHANES 1999-2004 (NT-proBNP, 11,370 adults). Models trained on 80% and evaluated on the remaining 20%. For each variable the model gives its full distribution: median, 80% interval and probability of crossing each clinical threshold.

- **CRPS**: error of the predicted distribution, in the variable's units (lower is better). **Improvement over population** = 1 - CRPS / CRPS of predicting the whole population's distribution.
- **80% coverage**: share of patients whose actual value falls in the 80% interval (should be around 0.80).
- NHANES urine is only albumin, creatinine, volume and flow: there is no dipstick (glucose, protein, blood, specific gravity), which in practice would add information.
