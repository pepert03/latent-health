# Lives the method would save in Spain: an estimate built on assumptions

Generated by `scripts/vidas_salvadas.py`. **This is not a measured result**: it is a chain of assumptions to give the order of magnitude. Each link states where it comes from.

## The pathway counted: hidden albuminuria treated with SGLT2 inhibitors

- **Baseline mortality (data):** of the 1854 people aged 40 or older with diabetes or hypertension and albuminuria in NHANES 1999-2008, 19.4% died of heart disease or stroke within 10 years (2.14% per year). With the same diabetes or hypertension and no albuminuria, 9.4%.
- **Treatment effect (published):** SGLT2 inhibitors reduce cardiovascular death by 14% (RR 0.86; interval 0.81 to 0.92; meta-analysis of 13 trials and 90,409 people, Lancet 2022;400:1788).

## What is not counted

- **Hidden diabetes.** Finding it earlier has not been shown to reduce 5-10 year mortality (ADDITION trial); its benefit comes through the kidney and heart pathway, already counted above.
- **Atrial fibrillation.** Screening has not clearly been shown to reduce strokes or deaths (LOOP, STROKESTOP).
- **Low ejection fraction.** Treatment saves lives in people with symptoms (DAPA-HF: death 11.6% vs 13.9% at 18 months), but the ECG only reaches people with a watch or a prior heart attack: tens of lives a year, not hundreds, on even weaker assumptions.
- **Kidney deaths** (dialysis averted) and heart failure admissions, which SGLT2 inhibitors also reduce.

For comparison: 113,620 people die each year in Spain from diseases of the circulatory system (INE, 2024).
