# External validation in Spaniards: PREDIMED trial sample

Generated by `scripts/validate_predimed.py`. 6324 participants from the PREDIMED trial (85% of the trial, chosen at random; Spanish primary care, 2003-2010), all at high cardiovascular risk and free of cardiovascular disease at entry. Mean age 67 years, 58% women, 47% with diabetes. Over a mean follow-up of 4.4 years there were 252 heart attacks, strokes or cardiovascular deaths.

The model is trained on cardiovascular deaths in the US (NHANES 1999-2014, 17,848 people aged 45 or older without prior cardiovascular disease) using only what PREDIMED also has: age, sex, smoking, weight, waist, and diagnoses of diabetes, hypertension and high cholesterol. PREDIMED plays no part in training.

Concordance index (probability of assigning higher risk to whoever has the event first), with its 95% interval:

| Model | Concordance index in PREDIMED |
|---|---|
| Age and sex (trained in the US) | 0.631 (0.59-0.67) |
| History (trained in the US) | 0.662 (0.63-0.69) |
| Age and sex (trained in PREDIMED) | 0.627 (0.59-0.66) |
| History (trained in PREDIMED) | 0.649 (0.62-0.68) |

- What history adds to age and sex, with the US model: +0.030 (0.011 to 0.051).
- What is gained by training within PREDIMED instead of bringing over the US model: -0.013 (-0.043 to 0.015).

## How to read it

- **All the indices are low, as expected.** PREDIMED only admitted high-risk people (diabetes or three risk factors) aged 55 to 80: since they are so alike, any model struggles to rank them.
- **The most informative data are missing**: no urine, no blood pressure, no medications, no prior heart disease (excluded by design). This validation only says whether the little they share carries over to Spaniards.
- The outcome includes non-fatal heart attacks and strokes, which the model never saw in training.
- It is a trial with an intervention (Mediterranean diet) that reduced events in two of the three arms.
