# Home kit: what is lost by removing each piece

Generated by `scripts/fit_kit.py`. Same data, people and outcome as `reports/riesgo_en_casa.md`: 12,710 people aged 40 or older from NHANES 1999-2008, 701 deaths from heart disease or stroke over 10 years. Five-fold cross-validation: each person is predicted by a model that did not see them.

The question is whether the kit can carry fewer things **without ranking risk worse**. The strip is the project's own (albumin and creatinine, read with the phone, with its simulated error).

## AUC with each combination

| What is used | Everyone | No prior cardiovascular disease | With diabetes or hypertension | Deaths in the top 20% risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Full kit: history, five questions, BP cuff and strip | 0.844 | 0.840 | 0.805 | 70% |
| Kit for everyone: history, five questions and strip (no BP cuff) | 0.844 | 0.840 | 0.807 | 70% |
| History and strip (no questions or BP cuff) | 0.841 | 0.837 | 0.801 | 69% |
| History and five questions (no strip or BP cuff) | 0.838 | 0.833 | 0.798 | 70% |
| Clinical history only | 0.837 | 0.832 | 0.795 | 70% |

## Difference from the full kit

In the same people, with the 95% bootstrap interval. If the interval includes zero, removing those pieces does not measurably change the risk ranking.

| What is used, minus the full kit | Everyone | No prior cardiovascular disease | With diabetes or hypertension |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kit for everyone: history, five questions and strip (no BP cuff) | +0.000 (-0.003 to 0.003) | -0.000 (-0.004 to 0.004) | +0.002 (-0.003 to 0.006) |
| History and strip (no questions or BP cuff) | -0.004 (-0.008 to 0.000) | -0.003 (-0.010 to 0.003) | -0.004 (-0.010 to 0.002) |
| History and five questions (no strip or BP cuff) | -0.006 (-0.010 to -0.002) | -0.007 (-0.013 to -0.000) | -0.007 (-0.014 to -0.000) |
| Clinical history only | -0.008 (-0.013 to -0.002) | -0.008 (-0.016 to 0.001) | -0.011 (-0.020 to -0.002) |

## How to read it

- This measures **risk ranking**, not diagnosis. The BP cuff remains the way to diagnose hypertension at home and the glucometer the way to find hidden diabetes (`reports/diabetes_en_casa.md`): they are lent to those whose record calls for them, not to everyone.
- The five questions cost nothing, so they stay even though their contribution is small.
- US population, blood pressure taken by trained staff and lab urine with simulated strip error: the same limits as `reports/riesgo_en_casa.md`.
