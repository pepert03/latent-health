# Atrial fibrillation detector from beat-to-beat intervals

Generated by `scripts/fit_rhythm.py`. Data: PhysioNet/CinC Challenge 2017, 8528 recordings of 9 to 60 seconds from a single-lead pocket ECG device, labeled by experts: normal rhythm 5076, atrial fibrillation 758, other rhythm 2415, noise 279. Five-fold cross-validation.

The detector does not look at the waveform: it detects each beat and measures how irregular the intervals between them are (17 measures). That way it works with anything that marks the beat, including the phone camera. The threshold is set for 95% specificity on the training data of each fold.

## Results

| Input | AUC vs everything else | AUC vs normal rhythm | Sensitivity | Specificity | Normals flagged | "Other rhythm" flagged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| ECG beats | 0.971 | 0.988 | 87.5% | 93.8% | 1.8% | 13.0% |
| Beats degraded like a camera's (simulated) | 0.957 | 0.979 | 83.1% | 93.5% | 2.1% | 13.3% |
| Trained on ECG, used with camera (simulated) | 0.941 | 0.962 | 90.5% | 80.0% | 12.9% | 32.8% |
| Simple rule: interval variability | 0.751 | 0.823 | 5.4% | 95.0% | 2.2% | 6.1% |

Recordings with fewer than 8 valid intervals, on which the detector gives no opinion: 0 (0.0%).

- **"Other rhythm" flagged** is the real cost: ectopic beats and other arrhythmias also produce an irregular pulse, and intervals alone cannot always tell them apart. These people are not healthy either: a doctor should see them too.
- **The camera rows are simulated**: the same beats with 20 ms of jitter and 3% dropped. There are no openly available labeled camera recordings to test it for real.

## What each detector result means

Observed atrial fibrillation by the probability the detector gives, and how much it multiplies the prior odds (likelihood ratio). This is what the patient record uses to update its probability.

| Detector probability | Recordings | Mean predicted | Observed fibrillation | Likelihood ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0% to 2% | 6429 | 0.3% | 0.4% | 0.0 |
| 2% to 10% | 891 | 4.6% | 7.3% | 0.8 |
| 10% to 30% | 396 | 17.6% | 19.9% | 2.6 |
| 30% to 70% | 295 | 47.9% | 44.7% | 8.3 |
| 70% to 100% | 517 | 88.5% | 88.0% | 75.2 |

## Limits

- **This is not the screening population.** These are recordings from device users, with 9% fibrillation; in screening of people over 65 undiagnosed fibrillation is 1-2%. That is why the patient record does not use the probability as is: it converts it into a likelihood ratio and applies it to the Spanish prevalence by age and sex.
- **Short, single-moment recordings.** Paroxysmal fibrillation may not be present at that moment.
- **The best methods in the challenge use the waveform** and deep networks; this one uses only intervals so it can work with the camera. It does not try to beat them.
- It is unknown whether there are several recordings from the same person; if so, the cross-validation is somewhat optimistic.
