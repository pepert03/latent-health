# Albuminuria filter validation (NHANES 2009-2010)

Generated by `scripts/validate_albuminuria.py`. Model fitted on the training split only (4437 people); here it is evaluated on the 976 test people with both samples (random at the health center and first-morning urine at home).

## Fitted parameters

- Excess of the random sample over the first-morning one (no elevation): x1.23 at 3 mg/g, x1.36 at 10 mg/g, x1.49 at 30 mg/g, x1.65 at 100 mg/g, x1.81 at 300 mg/g.
- Usual per-sample variation (log scale, Student's t with ν = 8.7): first-morning 0.274, random 0.302.
- Transient elevations (50-year-old man without diabetes, eGFR 90, urine of typical dilution): 15% of random samples and 7% of first-morning ones, x2.1 on average (ω = 0.61 in log). Effects on the logit: age/15 years +0.11, woman +0.81, diabetes +0.01, eGFR/20 -0.04, dilution (log urine creatinine) -0.14.
- Effects on the variation scale (log, per unit): age/15 years -0.04, woman -0.11, diabetes +0.09, eGFR/20 -0.09, dilution -0.19.
- Test log-likelihood per person (higher is better): full -1.785, light-tailed elevations -1.789, no sample covariates -1.798, no increasing excess -1.802, no elevations -1.825, normal noise -1.931.
- Dynamics (not fitted with these data): true change of 0.35 in log per year (≈ ±42%).
