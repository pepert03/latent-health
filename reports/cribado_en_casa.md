# Home albuminuria screening: protocol simulation

Generated by `scripts/simulate_home.py`. 1500 simulated people with the real covariates of NHANES 2009-2010 (aged 60 or older, or with diabetes). True albuminuria (UACR ≥ 30 mg/g) in 10.1%. Each policy is tested on the same mornings for each person.

- **PPV**: of the people flagged as positive, how many truly have albuminuria.
- **True positives detected**: of those with albuminuria, how many end up flagged (pending the three-month repeat that chronicity requires).
- **Unresolved**: finish the kit still in doubt and are referred to the health center.

## Filter with the two-stage protocol

The kit comes with 3 strips and allows up to 2 lab tubes. It moves to the lab tube when the strips run out or when one more strip would resolve the doubt with probability below 30%.

| Policy | PPV | True positives detected | True positives ruled out | Unresolved | Strips per person | Lab tubes per person |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strips read with the phone, lab tube if in doubt | 98% | 81% | 4.0% | 3.1% | 1.17 | 0.13 |
| Phone-read strips only (up to 6, no lab tube) | 98% | 78% | 6.0% | 3.5% | 1.39 | 0.00 |
| 3-category strip, lab tube if in doubt | 98% | 85% | 8.6% | 1.8% | 1.08 | 0.13 |
| Strips read by eye, lab tube if in doubt | 97% | 82% | 4.0% | 3.1% | 1.19 | 0.15 |
| Mailed lab tube only (up to 2) | 99% | 73% | 2.6% | 5.6% | 0.00 | 1.11 |

## Rule-out threshold

Strips let albuminuria near 30 mg/g slip through: with one negative strip the probability drops below 5% and the protocol ends. The threshold is a utility decision (`decision.Utilities`): if not missing an albuminuria is worth 50 tests instead of 20, it rules out only below 2%.

| Policy | PPV | True positives detected | True positives ruled out | Unresolved | Strips per person | Lab tubes per person |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Rule out below 5% (default) | 98% | 81% | 4.0% | 3.1% | 1.17 | 0.13 |
| Rule out below 2% | 98% | 81% | 4.0% | 4.1% | 1.27 | 0.17 |

## Reading color continuously with a vision model

Each pad's color changes gradually with concentration; the 4 or 5 levels are for the eye. A vision model could read the hue continuously. Here it is simulated as a strip with many levels within the same chemical range (the pad still saturates outside 10-150 mg/l), first with the same reading error as today's photo and then with half of it.

| Policy | PPV | True positives detected | True positives ruled out | Unresolved | Strips per person | Lab tubes per person |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Strip levels read with the phone (today) | 98% | 81% | 4.0% | 3.1% | 1.17 | 0.13 |
| Color read continuously, same reading error | 98% | 83% | 4.6% | 3.1% | 1.18 | 0.12 |
| Color read continuously, half the error | 99% | 85% | 4.0% | 2.7% | 1.16 | 0.10 |
| Reference: lab tube | 99% | 73% | 2.6% | 5.6% | 0.00 | 1.11 |

## Context questions before the test

In the simulated world, intense exercise, fever, menstruation and infection symptoms multiply the probability of a transient elevation (assumed effects; see `albuminuria/context.py`). Without questions, the filter does not know which mornings have a cause. With questions, the test is postponed on those mornings and the filter knows the ones that go ahead do not have one.

| Policy | PPV | True positives detected | True positives ruled out | Unresolved | Strips per person | Lab tubes per person |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| No questions | 98% | 81% | 4.0% | 3.1% | 1.17 | 0.13 |
| With questions | 98% | 81% | 5.3% | 3.0% | 1.16 | 0.14 |

Days to finish (mean): 1.30 without questions and 1.94 with them (postponing costs days).

## Limits

- This is a simulation within the model: it assumes the measurement model fitted on NHANES is correct and that a person's mornings are independent of each other given their true level.
- The strip reading error (phone, by eye, ratio) is an assumption. It must be measured with samples read with the strip and in the lab at the same time (`fit_strip_spec`).
- The effect of the context questions is an assumption; the table says how much would be gained if it held.
- It does not simulate the three-month repeat or dropping out halfway through the kit.
