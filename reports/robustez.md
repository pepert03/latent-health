## 1. eGFR and albuminuria together

979 test people from NHANES 2009-2010. The system receives the clinical record and the random urine sample, without the blood test. There are 63 people with eGFR < 60, 47 with albuminuria, 6 with both and 27 at high or very high KDIGO risk: with so few joint cases, small differences between rows mean nothing. Predictions are compared with the person's actual eGFR and their first-morning urine (a single sample, so as an albuminuria reference it is noisy; it affects the three ways of combining equally).
