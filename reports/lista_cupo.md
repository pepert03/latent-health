# Worklist: validation with real people

Generated by `scripts/validate_worklist.py`. People aged 40 or older from the NHANES 2009-2010 test split, with first-morning urine as the albuminuria reference (a single sample: it is noisy). Models not recalibrated to Spain, because the reference is NHANES.

The system receives the clinical record and gives the probability that completing the tests will yield a new finding. The list is ranked by that probability.

## Limits

- US general population, not a Spanish practice list; few people, so the top-10% figures have a wide margin of error.
- In situation B, SCORE2 is approximated with typical lipids; what is recorded is that approximation.
- The albuminuria reference is a single morning sample, not confirmed at three months.
