# Recalibrating the priors to Spanish primary care

Generated by `scripts/fit_recalibration.py`. Reference: ONDAAS, Clin Kidney J 2025;18(5):sfaf123 (Burgos, February-May 2024), 9526 adults (≥ 18 years) whose family doctor ordered a lab test. Comparison population: the 25,319 adults in NHANES 2009-2018 reweighted to the ONDAAS age and sex distribution.

Only intercepts move. The urine measurement model and the way each piece of patient data changes the estimate stay as they were.

## Albuminuria

Share of people whose urine sample comes out ≥ 30 mg/g, before measuring anything.

| | ONDAAS | NHANES model | Recalibrated model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Total | 14.0% | 15.1% | 14.0% |
| 18-44 years | 3.0% | 5.1% | 4.6% |
| 45-64 years | 8.2% | 11.5% | 10.6% |
| 65+ years | 20.6% | 20.6% | 19.3% |

Constant added to the logit of the prior's elevated components: **-0.187** (multiplies the odds of albuminuria by 0.83). The NHANES prior was already close. Only the total is fitted; the age rows are a check, not a target.

The ONDAAS urine is assumed to be a random sample (it was collected at the health center). If it were first-morning urine, the NHANES model would give 9.6% and the constant would be +1.235.

## eGFR

ONDAAS used the CKD-EPI 2009 equation and this project uses the 2021 one, which gives somewhat higher values for the same creatinine. To compare like with like, each category is computed with the 2009 cutoff translated to the 2021 scale person by person (eGFR 60 under 2009 equals 61 to 65 under 2021).

| Category (2009 equation) | ONDAAS | NHANES panel | Recalibrated panel |
|---|---|---|---|
| eGFR < 60 | 12.8% | 18.3% | 12.8% |
| G1 | 36.4% | 40.2% | 41.8% |
| G2 | 50.8% | 41.5% | 45.4% |
| G3a | 8.3% | 11.4% | 8.6% |
| G3b | 3.6% | 4.7% | 2.9% |
| G4 | 0.9% | 1.6% | 1.0% |
| G5 | 0.0% | 0.5% | 0.3% |

Constant added to the logit of eGFR < 60 in the panel distribution: **-0.779** (multiplies its odds by 0.46). Only eGFR < 60 is fitted; the other categories are a check. The G1/G2 split is not corrected: it changes no decision.

## Caveats

- ONDAAS are patients who had a lab test ordered, not the whole practice list.
- The paper does not say whether the urine was first-morning; a random sample is assumed.
- ONDAAS used CKD-EPI 2009 and Jaffe creatinine; the target is translated to the CKD-EPI 2021 scale.
- The comparison population is NHANES reweighted by age and sex; NHANES age is capped at 80.
- This is a population correction, not a validation: there are no individual Spanish data to check calibration patient by patient.
